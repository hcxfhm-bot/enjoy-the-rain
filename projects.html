15 Beginner 3D Printing Projects You Can Make Today

📅 Updated April 2026 · ⏱️ 7 min read

Got your printer set up? Here are 15 projects perfect for beginners — from useful household items to fun decorations. All models are free to download.

📢 Advertisement

🏠 Useful Household Prints

1. Phone Stand

The perfect first project. Simple, fast (30 min), and genuinely useful. Search "phone stand" on Thingiverse — there are hundreds of designs for every phone model.

  • ⏱️ Print time: 30-60 min
  • 🧵 Material: 10g PLA
  • 🎯 Difficulty: ⭐ Easy

2. Cable Management Clips

Organize the mess of cables behind your desk. These little clips stick to your desk edge with double-sided tape and hold cables in place.

  • ⏱️ Print time: 15 min each
  • 🧵 Material: 3g PLA
  • 🎯 Difficulty: ⭐ Easy

3. Headphone Hook / Hanger

Clamp-on or stick-on headphone hangers that attach to your desk. Saves space and looks professional.

  • ⏱️ Print time: 1-2 hours
  • 🧵 Material: 20g PLA/PLA+
  • 🎯 Difficulty: ⭐ Easy

4. Key Holder / Wall Hook

Customizable key holders for your entryway. Print your family name or a fun design.

  • ⏱️ Print time: 1-2 hours
  • 🧵 Material: 30g PLA
  • 🎯 Difficulty: ⭐⭐

5. Spool Holder / Filament Guide

A better filament spool holder than the one that came with your printer. Smoother unwinding = fewer print failures.

  • ⏱️ Print time: 2-4 hours
  • 🧵 Material: 50g PLA/PETG
  • 🎯 Difficulty: ⭐⭐
📢 Advertisement

🌱 Garden & Outdoor

6. Self-Watering Planter

A two-part planter with a water reservoir at the bottom. Perfect for herbs and small plants. Print in PETG for outdoor use.

  • ⏱️ Print time: 4-6 hours
  • 🧵 Material: 80g PETG
  • 🎯 Difficulty: ⭐⭐

7. Bird Feeder

A simple hanging bird feeder — fun to watch and great for the garden.

  • ⏱️ Print time: 5-8 hours
  • 🧵 Material: 100g PETG
  • 🎯 Difficulty: ⭐⭐

🧸 Toys & Fun

8. Articulated Shark / Dragon / Octopus

These print-in-place articulated toys are a 3D printing rite of passage. No assembly required — they come off the bed fully movable!

  • ⏱️ Print time: 3-5 hours
  • 🧵 Material: 50g PLA (silk PLA looks great)
  • 🎯 Difficulty: ⭐⭐

9. Fidget Spinner / Infinity Cube

Print-in-place fidget toys. Satisfying to use and even more satisfying to make yourself.

  • ⏱️ Print time: 1-3 hours
  • 🧵 Material: 30g PLA
  • 🎯 Difficulty: ⭐⭐

10. Custom Chess Set

There are dozens of free chess set designs on Thingiverse and Printables. Print your own unique set — great gift idea.

  • ⏱️ Print time: Varies (each piece 1-2 hours)
  • 🧵 Material: 200g total PLA
  • 🎯 Difficulty: ⭐⭐

🔧 Functional Upgrades

11. Drawer Divider / Organizer

Custom-fit organizers for your desk drawers, kitchen, or toolbox. Measure your drawer, model (or find) the dividers, print.

  • ⏱️ Print time: 2-4 hours
  • 🧵 Material: 40g PLA
  • 🎯 Difficulty: ⭐⭐

12. Soldering Helping Hands / Tool Holders

If you do any electronics work, a 3D printed helping hands stand is a game-changer.

  • ⏱️ Print time: 2-3 hours
  • 🧵 Material: 60g PLA
  • 🎯 Difficulty: ⭐⭐

13. Test Print: 3DBenchy / Calibration Cube

Every beginner should print a Benchy (the little boat). It's designed to test every aspect of your printer: overhangs, bridging, dimension accuracy, surface finish.

  • ⏱️ Print time: 45-60 min
  • 🧵 Material: 12g PLA
  • 🎯 Difficulty: ⭐ Easy
📢 Advertisement

🎁 Gift Ideas

14. Personalized Keychain / Name Tag

Design or download a name tag. Use Tinkercad to add text. Makes a great personalized gift.

  • ⏱️ Print time: 15-30 min
  • 🧵 Material: 5g PLA
  • 🎯 Difficulty: ⭐ Easy

15. Custom Cookie Cutter

Design your own cookie cutter shapes! Just make sure to use food-safe PLA and wash thoroughly. Great for holidays.

  • ⏱️ Print time: 1 hour
  • 🧵 Material: 20g PLA
  • 🎯 Difficulty: ⭐⭐
💡 What to Print Next: Start with #1 (Phone Stand) and #13 (Benchy). They're quick wins that build confidence. Then tackle #8 (Articulated Toy) — it's incredibly satisfying.
