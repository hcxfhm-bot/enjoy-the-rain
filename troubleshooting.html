3D Printing Troubleshooting: Fix Every Common Problem

📅 Updated April 2026 · ⏱️ 10 min read

Every 3D printer owner runs into problems. The key is knowing why the problem happened and how to fix it — fast. This guide covers the 8 most common issues.

📢 Advertisement

#1: Print Not Sticking to the Bed

What you see: The first layer lifts off, curls up, or the print completely detaches mid-print.

This is the #1 beginner problem. Here's how to fix it:

  1. Clean the bed — Wipe with 90%+ isopropyl alcohol. Finger oils are invisible adhesion killers.
  2. Level your bed — Even with auto-leveling, a poorly trammed bed causes adhesion issues. Re-level manually.
  3. Adjust Z-offset — The nozzle is probably too far from the bed. Lower Z-offset by 0.05-0.1mm.
  4. Increase bed temperature — PLA: 60°C, PETG: 80°C
  5. Use adhesion aids — Glue stick (for PETG on glass), blue painter's tape, or PEI sheet upgrade
  6. Add a brim — In your slicer, add a 5-8mm brim for extra first-layer surface area
💡 Quick Fix: If nothing else works, slow down the first layer to 20mm/s and increase first layer height to 0.3mm. This alone fixes 50% of adhesion problems.

#2: Stringing / Oozing

What you see: Thin plastic strands between parts of your print, like spider webs.

  1. Enable retraction — In Cura, set Retraction Distance to 5-6mm (Bowden) or 1-2mm (Direct Drive)
  2. Lower nozzle temperature — Try dropping 5°C at a time. Less heat = less oozing.
  3. Increase travel speed — Set travel speed to 150-200mm/s
  4. Dry your filament — Wet filament strings more

#3: Warping / Curling

What you see: The bottom corners of your print curl upward, lifting off the bed.

  1. Use a heated bed — PLA at 60°C is the minimum
  2. Eliminate drafts — Turn off fans, close windows, avoid AC vents near the printer
  3. Add a brim or raft — Gives the corners something to hold onto
  4. Use an enclosure — Even a cardboard box over the printer helps for ABS/PETG
  5. Reduce infill — Less internal plastic = less shrinkage force

#4: Layer Shifting

What you see: The print suddenly shifts horizontally, like a misaligned stack of pancakes.

  1. Check belt tension — Belts should be tight like a guitar string, not loose
  2. Check for obstructions — Make sure cables aren't snagging
  3. Lower acceleration & jerk — Start with acceleration at 500mm/s²
  4. Check stepper drivers — Overheating drivers cause missed steps

#5: Blobs & Zits

What you see: Random bumps and blobs on the surface of your print.

  1. Dry your filament — Moisture popping causes blobs
  2. Adjust retraction — Too much retraction can cause blobs on resume
  3. Disable "Power Loss Recovery" — On some printers, this causes micro-pauses
  4. Reduce print speed — If your printer's processor can't keep up

#6: Under-Extrusion

What you see: Thin layers, gaps in walls, weak and brittle prints.

  1. Check for clogs — Perform a cold pull to clean the nozzle
  2. Check extruder tension — The extruder gear should grip the filament firmly
  3. Check filament diameter — Should be 1.75mm (measure with calipers)
  4. Calibrate E-steps — Mark 100mm of filament, tell printer to extrude 100mm, measure actual
  5. Increase flow rate — In slicer, bump flow to 105%

#7: Overheating / Poor Overhangs

What you see: Droopy bridges, curled overhangs, melted-looking details.

  1. Improve cooling — Make sure the part cooling fan is at 100% for PLA
  2. Print a temperature tower — Find the lowest temp that still works for your filament
  3. Print cooler — Drop nozzle temp 5-10°C
  4. Slow down for overhangs — Set overhang speed to 50% in your slicer

#8: Elephant's Foot

What you see: The bottom layers bulge outward more than the rest of the print.

  1. Lower bed temperature — Drop 5°C at a time (PLA: 50°C may work)
  2. Adjust Z-offset — You might be too close to the bed
  3. Use "Initial Layer Horizontal Expansion" — Set to -0.15mm in Cura
  4. Add a chamfer — If designing your own models, add a 0.5mm chamfer to bottom edges

When to Ask for Help

Some problems are beyond basic troubleshooting. If you've tried every fix and nothing works, post in r/3Dprinting or r/FixMyPrint on Reddit. Include a clear photo and details about your printer, filament, and settings.

The 3D printing community is incredibly helpful — don't be shy about asking!
