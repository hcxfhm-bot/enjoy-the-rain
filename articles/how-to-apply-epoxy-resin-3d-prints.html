How to Apply Epoxy Resin to 3D Prints for a Pro Finish

📅 June 1, 2026⏱️ 6 min read✍️ 3D Print Starter Team
💎

Epoxy resin coating is the secret to making 3D prints look like commercial products. A thin layer of epoxy fills layer lines, adds a hard protective shell, and creates a deep glossy finish that paint alone can't match.

Why Use Epoxy on 3D Prints?

  • Eliminates layer lines completely — fills even deep valleys
  • Adds strength — the epoxy shell makes prints significantly more rigid
  • Waterproofs — sealed surfaces won't absorb moisture
  • Professional look — deep, glass-like gloss that looks factory-finished

Recommended Products

  • XTC-3D by Smooth-On — Designed specifically for 3D prints, self-leveling, 2-part mix
  • ArtResin — Crystal clear, UV resistant, food-safe when cured
  • West System 105 — Industrial grade, maximum strength

Application Steps

  1. Sand the print with 220 grit — epoxy needs a slightly rough surface to adhere
  2. Clean thoroughly — wipe with isopropyl alcohol to remove dust and oils
  3. Mix epoxy — Follow the exact ratio (usually 2:1 or 1:1 by volume)
  4. Apply thin coat — Use a foam brush, work quickly (15 min pot life)
  5. Rotate for even coverage — Turn the part to prevent drips and pooling
  6. Cure for 24 hours — Keep dust-free during curing
  7. Light sand and second coat — For ultra-smooth results, add 2-3 thin coats

For other finishing methods, check our painting guide and vapor smoothing guide.
