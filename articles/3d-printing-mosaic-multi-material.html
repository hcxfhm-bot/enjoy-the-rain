Key Consideration: Waste

Single-nozzle multi-material systems generate waste during filament changes. A typical 4-color print wastes 20-40% of filament as purge material. IDEX doesn't have this problem since each nozzle has its own filament.

When Multi-Material Is Worth It

  • Color signage and labels (text in different color)
  • Flexible + rigid combinations (TPU gasket + PLA body)
  • Dissolvable support (PLA + PVA)
  • Prototypes requiring color-coded sections
