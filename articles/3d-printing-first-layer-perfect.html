How to Get the Perfect First Layer Every Time: Complete Guide

📅 June 1, 2026⏱️ 8 min read✍️ 3D Print Starter Team
🎯

If your first layer is right, the rest of the print usually works. If it's wrong, nothing saves the print. Here's the complete guide to getting a perfect first layer every single time.

First Layer Visual Guide

SymptomCauseFix
Lines don't touch (gaps)Nozzle too far from bedLower Z-offset by 0.05mm
Rough, scalloped surfaceNozzle too close to bedRaise Z-offset by 0.05mm
Elephant foot (flared base)Too close + too hot bedRaise Z-offset slightly, lower bed temp
Corner liftingAdhesion failureAdd brim, clean bed, increase bed temp
Skirt doesn't stickBed not level or not cleanRe-level, wash bed with soap

The 5-Step First Layer Protocol

  1. Clean the bed — Wash with warm water and dish soap. No IPA for first-time prep.
  2. Level the bed — At printing temperature, all 4 corners, paper test.
  3. Set Z-offset — Print a first layer test square. Adjust until lines are smooth and fused.
  4. Choose adhesion helper — Brim for tall objects, glue stick for PETG, nothing for clean PLA on PEI.
  5. Watch the first layer — Never walk away until the first layer is complete.

First Layer Settings

  • Speed: 15-20 mm/s (half your normal speed)
  • Flow: 100-105% (slight over-extrusion helps adhesion)
  • Bed temp: PLA 60°C, PETG 80°C, ABS 100°C
  • Fan: Off for first 2-3 layers
