Add a Camera to Your 3D Printer: Raspberry Pi & OctoPrint

📅 June 1, 2026⏱️ 7 min read✍️ 3D Print Starter Team
📷

A camera lets you monitor your prints from anywhere, create time-lapses, and catch failures early. Here's how to set up camera monitoring for your 3D printer.

OctoPrint Camera Setup

  1. Install OctoPrint on your Raspberry Pi
  2. Connect the camera (USB or CSI ribbon)
  3. Install the OctoPrint Webcam plugin
  4. Configure in Settings → Webcam & Timelapse
  5. Enable "Timelapse" for automatic recording

Camera Angle Tips

  • Side view — Best for seeing print quality and layer adhesion
  • Front view — Best for monitoring overall progress
  • Overhead — Best for time-lapse videos
  • Mount the camera on a tripod or print a custom mount
