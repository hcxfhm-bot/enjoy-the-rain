How to Mix and Handle Resin for 3D Printing Safely

📅 June 1, 2026⏱️ 6 min read✍️ 3D Print Starter Team
🧤

Resin printing requires careful handling. Uncured resin is toxic to skin and lungs, and improper mixing leads to failed prints. Here's how to handle resin safely and effectively.

Required PPE

  • Nitrile gloves — Always wear when handling resin
  • Safety glasses — Prevent splashes in eyes
  • Respirator/mask — N95 minimum when pouring or cleaning
  • Apron — Protect clothing from spills

Mixing and Pouring

  1. Shake the bottle for 2-3 minutes before use — pigments and additives settle
  2. Let it rest for 10 minutes after shaking — bubbles need time to rise
  3. Pour slowly down the side of the vat — minimizes bubbles
  4. Fill to the line — Don't overfill or underfill the vat

Storage

  • Store in a cool, dark place (resin degrades in UV light)
  • Keep bottles tightly sealed (resin absorbs moisture)
  • Label with purchase date — most resins last 12-18 months
  • Never pour resin back into the bottle after it's been in the vat
