Glow-in-the-Dark Filament Guide: Settings, Tips & Best Brands

📅 June 1, 2026⏱️ 6 min read✍️ 3D Print Starter Team
🌟

Glow-in-the-dark filament is one of the most fun specialty materials you can print with. It charges under UV light and emits a soft glow in darkness — perfect for night lights, keychains, gaming accessories, and decorative pieces.

How It Works

GITD filament contains strontium aluminate particles mixed into a PLA base. Strontium aluminate is 10x brighter and lasts 10x longer than the older zinc sulfide pigments. Modern GITD filament can glow for 8-12 hours after just 5-10 minutes of UV exposure.

Print Settings

  • Nozzle: 200-220 °C (PLA base)
  • Bed: 50-60 °C
  • Speed: 30-50 mm/s
  • Nozzle: 0.6 mm recommended (GITD particles are abrasive and large)
  • Wall count: 3+ (thicker walls = more glow material = brighter)

Tips for Maximum Glow

  • Print with 100% infill or at least 50% — more material means brighter glow
  • Use a 0.6 mm nozzle — the glow particles are larger than standard pigments
  • Expose to sunlight or a UV flashlight for fastest charging
  • Hardened steel nozzle recommended — strontium aluminate is mildly abrasive
