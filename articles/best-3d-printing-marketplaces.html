Best 3D Printing Marketplaces to Sell Your Models in 2026

📅 June 1, 2026⏱️ 7 min read✍️ 3D Print Starter Team
🏪

Selling 3D models and prints has never been easier, but choosing the right marketplace makes a huge difference in your success. Here's a comparison of the top platforms in 2026.

Marketplace Comparison

PlatformWhat You SellFeesAudience
EtsyPhysical prints6.5% + $0.20/listingMassive, general consumers
PrintablesDigital models (free)Free to listLarge, maker community
Cults3DDigital models (paid)10-15%Medium, hobbyists
ThangsDigital models (paid)10%Growing, search-focused
MyMiniFactoryDigital models (paid/free)10-15%Medium, quality-focused
ShapewaysPhysical prints (they print)Varies by materialLarge, professional

Best Strategy: Multi-Platform

Don't rely on one platform. The most successful sellers list on 3-4 marketplaces simultaneously:

  • Digital models: Cults3D + Thangs + MyMiniFactory
  • Physical prints: Etsy + local classifieds
  • Free models: Printables (drives traffic to paid content)
