3D Printing Essentials Checklist: What to Buy Before Your First Print

📅 June 1, 2026⏱️ 6 min read✍️ 3D Print Starter Team
✅

You bought a printer, but you'll need a few more things before your first print is successful. Here's your essential checklist.

Must-Have (Before First Print)

ItemWhyCost
PLA filamentEasiest material to start with$15-25/kg
SD card (if not included)Transfer files to printer$8
Isopropyl alcohol 70%+Clean build plate$5
Paper (for bed leveling)Standard feeler gauge$0
Glue stick (purple)Backup adhesion helper$2

Should-Have (First Week)

ItemWhyCost
Scraper/spatulaRemove prints from bed$5
Side cutters/flush cuttersRemove supports$8
Needle nose pliersGeneral purpose$8
Calipers (digital)Measure dimensions$10-15
Extra nozzles (0.4mm)Clogs happen$5 for 5-pack

Nice-to-Have (First Month)

  • Filament dryer or storage boxes with silica gel
  • Set of Allen keys (if not included)
  • Blue painter's tape (alternative bed surface)
  • Small needle/pin for nozzle cleaning
