Metallic Filament Guide: Print Stunning Metal-Like Objects

📅 June 1, 2026⏱️ 7 min read✍️ 3D Print Starter Team
✨

Metallic filament lets you print objects that look and feel like cast metal — without a foundry. Modern metal-filled filaments contain real metal powder mixed with a PLA or ABS base, producing stunning results for decorative pieces, jewelry, and display models.

How Metallic Filament Works

Metal-filled filaments are typically 60-80% PLA and 20-40% metal powder (copper, brass, bronze, steel, or aluminum). The metal particles give the printed part a genuine metallic weight, sheen, and cold-to-the-touch feel.

Recommended Settings

SettingValue
Nozzle Temperature200-220 °C (PLA base)
Bed Temperature50-60 °C
Print Speed30-50 mm/s (slower than regular PLA)
RetractionReduce by 20% vs normal PLA
Nozzle0.6 mm minimum (metal particles clog 0.4 mm nozzles)

Post-Processing for Real Metal Look

  1. Sand with 220 → 400 → 800 → 2000 grit progressively
  2. Polish with steel wool or polishing compound
  3. Patina (optional) — Apply liver of sulfur for brass/bronze to create an aged look
  4. Seal with clear spray to prevent oxidation

After sanding and polishing, the result looks like solid metal — most people can't tell it's 3D printed.
