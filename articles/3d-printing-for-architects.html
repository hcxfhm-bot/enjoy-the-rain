3D Printing for Architects: Scale Models and Prototyping

📅 June 1, 2026⏱️ 7 min read✍️ 3D Print Starter Team
🏛️

Architectural model-making has been transformed by 3D printing. What used to take weeks of hand-cutting foam and cardboard now takes hours of print time. Here's how architects are leveraging 3D printing in 2026.

FDM vs Resin for Architecture

FeatureFDMResin
Detail LevelMedium (0.1mm layers)High (0.025mm layers)
Build VolumeLarge (300mm+)Small-Medium (200mm)
Material OptionsMany colors, transparentLimited colors, clear available
Cost per Model$2-20$5-30
Best ForMassing models, site contextFine detail, facades, interiors

Workflow: CAD to Physical Model

  1. Export from Revit/Rhino/SketchUp as STL
  2. Slice with appropriate settings (0.15mm for detail, 0.25mm for massing)
  3. Print in sections if model exceeds build volume
  4. Assemble with CA glue and touch up
