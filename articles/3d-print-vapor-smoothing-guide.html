Vapor Smoothing 3D Prints: How to Get a Glass-Like Finish

📅 June 1, 2026⏱️ 7 min read✍️ 3D Print Starter Team
✨

Vapor smoothing is a chemical finishing technique that uses solvent vapor to melt the surface of a 3D print, creating a smooth, glossy finish that eliminates layer lines entirely. It's the closest you can get to injection-molded quality from an FDM printer.

Materials That Can Be Vapor Smoothed

MaterialSolventResult
ABSAcetone⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Excellent — glossy glass finish
ASAAcetone⭐⭐⭐⭐ Very good
PLANot practical (ethyl acetate works poorly)⭐ Not recommended
PolycarbonateDichloromethane (dangerous)⭐⭐ Expert only

Acetone Vapor Smoothing for ABS (Step by Step)

  1. Prepare a sealed container — A glass jar with a tight lid works
  2. Add acetone — Pour 1-2 tablespoons into the bottom (NOT touching the print)
  3. Suspend the print — Use wire or a platform so the print doesn't touch the liquid
  4. Heat gently — Place the jar in a warm water bath (50-60°C) to increase vapor production
  5. Wait 10-30 minutes — Check every 5 minutes. The surface should look wet and glossy
  6. Remove and air out — Let the print dry for 1-2 hours in a well-ventilated area

Safety Warnings

  • Acetone is extremely flammable — No open flames, sparks, or heat guns
  • Work in a well-ventilated area — Acetone vapors cause headaches and dizziness
  • Wear nitrile gloves — Acetone absorbs through skin
  • Never microwave acetone — This creates an explosion risk
