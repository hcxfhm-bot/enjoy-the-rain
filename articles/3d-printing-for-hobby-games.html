3D Printing for Tabletop Games: Miniatures, Terrain & Accessories

📅 June 1, 2026⏱️ 7 min read✍️ 3D Print Starter Team
🎲

3D printing has become an essential tool for tabletop gamers. Custom miniatures, terrain pieces, spell effects, and game accessories are all within reach. Here's how to get started.

What to Print

CategoryExamplesBest Printer Type
MiniaturesHeroes, monsters, NPCsResin (detail critical)
TerrainWalls, trees, buildingsFDM (size and durability)
Spell effectsFireballs, ice, barriersResin (fine details)
Dice towersCustom towers, traysFDM (functional)
StorageCard holders, token boxesFDM (utility)

Best Miniature Sources

  • HeroForge — Custom character creator, $8/mini STL
  • ezArmor/Eldritch Foundry — Custom miniatures
  • Thingiverse/Printables — Free community models
  • MyMiniFactory — Curated premium and free

Settings for FDM Miniatures

  • 0.08-0.12mm layer height
  • 0.2mm nozzle
  • Slow speed (20-30 mm/s)
  • Tree/organic supports

For more miniature tips, see our miniatures printer guide and painting guide.
