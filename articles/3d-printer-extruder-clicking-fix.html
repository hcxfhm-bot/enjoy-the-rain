3D Printer Extruder Clicking: Causes and Fixes

📅 June 1, 2026⏱️ 7 min read✍️ 3D Print Starter Team
🔊

That rhythmic clicking from your extruder is the sound of the drive gear slipping on filament. It means the extruder is trying to push filament but can't — and every click is a moment of under-extrusion. Here's how to diagnose and fix it.

Quick Diagnosis Flowchart

  1. Does filament come out when you push it manually by hand?
    • No → Nozzle clog or heat creep (skip to fix #1)
    • Yes → Extruder/tension issue (skip to fix #3)

Fix #1: Nozzle Clog

The most common cause. Filament gets stuck in the nozzle and the extruder can't push through.

  • Cold pull: Heat to 200°C, push filament through, cool to 90°C, yank firmly
  • Acupuncture needle: Insert from the bottom while hot to clear debris
  • Replace nozzle: If repeated clogs, the nozzle may be worn or damaged

Fix #2: Heat Creep

Heat travels too far up the hotend, softening filament before it reaches the melt zone. The soft filament gets jammed.

  • Ensure the heat sink fan is running at 100% at all times
  • Check that the heat break is properly seated (PTFE tube fully inserted)
  • Lower retraction distance (excessive retraction pulls molten filament into cold zones)
  • Print at lower speeds for the hotend's volumetric capacity

Fix #3: Extruder Tension

If the drive gear can't grip the filament firmly enough, it slips and clicks.

  • Tighten the tension screw on the extruder arm
  • Check that the drive gear teeth are clean (not filled with filament dust)
  • For Bowden: check that the PTFE tube is fully seated on both ends
