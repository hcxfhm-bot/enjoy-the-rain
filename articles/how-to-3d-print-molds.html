How to 3D Print Molds for Casting: Silicone & Resin

📅 June 1, 2026⏱️ 7 min read✍️ 3D Print Starter Team
🫧

3D printing molds for casting combines the best of both worlds: the design freedom of 3D printing with the material properties of cast materials (silicone, resin, metal, concrete). It's a powerful technique for small-batch production.

Mold Types

Mold TypeBest ForMaterial
Single-part open moldFlat-backed objects, coastersPLA or PETG
Two-part moldComplex 3D objectsPLA or PETG
Silicone mold (3D printed master)Flexible, reusable moldsPLA master + silicone
Lost-PLA castingMetal partsPLA (burned out)

Design Rules for Printed Molds

  • Add draft angles (2-5°) for easy part removal
  • Include pour holes and air vents
  • Seam line should be on a natural edge of the model
  • Wall thickness: 2-3mm minimum for rigidity
  • Apply mold release before pouring (or the part will stick permanently)

Post-Processing the Mold

Layer lines will transfer to your cast. For smooth casts:

  1. Sand the mold interior with 400+ grit
  2. Apply a primer/sealer (spray can works well)
  3. Coat with mold release agent
  4. Polish if needed for high-gloss casts

For more techniques, see our sanding guide and design guide.
