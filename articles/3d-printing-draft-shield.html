3D Printing Draft Shield: Prevent Warping on Tall Prints

📅 June 1, 2026⏱️ 5 min read✍️ 3D Print Starter Team
🛡️

Warping on tall prints is often caused by air currents (from windows, AC, or fans) hitting the warm print unevenly. A draft shield is a thin wall printed around your model that blocks these drafts and creates a mini-enclosure effect.

When to Use a Draft Shield

  • Printing ABS, ASA, or PC without an enclosure
  • Tall prints that warp at the top
  • Drafty room (near windows or AC vents)
  • Large flat prints prone to warping

How to Enable in Cura

  1. Go to Custom settings → Search "Draft Shield"
  2. Set "Enable Draft Shield" to Yes
  3. Choose type: "All" (full enclosure) or "Limited" (only around certain areas)

How to Enable in PrusaSlicer

Settings → Print Settings → Skirt and brim → Draft shield → Enable

Trade-offs

  • Pro: Reduces warping significantly, especially for ABS
  • Con: Adds print time (10-20% more) and uses extra filament
  • Tip: Remove the shield immediately after printing while it's still warm — it can fuse to the model if left to cool

For more warping solutions, see our warping guide and enclosure guide.
