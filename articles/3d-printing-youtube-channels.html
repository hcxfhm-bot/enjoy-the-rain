Best 3D Printing YouTube Channels to Follow in 2026

📅 June 1, 2026⏱️ 6 min read✍️ 3D Print Starter Team
📺

YouTube is one of the best resources for learning 3D printing — you can see techniques in action that are impossible to convey in text. Here are the channels worth your time in 2026.

Top Channels by Category

Reviews & Buying Advice

  • CNC Kitchen — Scientific testing of filaments, settings, and hardware. Data-driven, no fluff.
  • Tom's 3D — Honest printer reviews with real-world testing methodology.
  • Teaching Tech — Comprehensive reviews with extensive calibration tests.

Tutorials & Learning

  • 3D Printing Nerd — Approachable beginner tutorials and project ideas.
  • Maker's Muse — Design tips, CAD tutorials, creative applications.
  • Printed Wee — Miniature and tabletop gaming focused, great for resin printing.

Modding & Advanced

  • Voron Design — Build guides for Voron printers, Klipper configuration.
  • Chezlicious — Bambu Lab tips, AMS techniques, filament reviews.

Entertainment & Inspiration

  • I Like To Make Stuff — Not exclusively 3D printing, but great maker content.
  • Adam Savage's Tested — Props, cosplay, and creative uses of 3D printing.
