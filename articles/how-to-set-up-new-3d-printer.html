How to Set Up a New 3D Printer: Unboxing to First Print

📅 June 1, 2026⏱️ 8 min read✍️ 3D Print Starter Team
📦

Unboxing your first 3D printer is exciting, but rushing through setup leads to frustration. Take 30-60 minutes to set it up properly, and your first print will actually work.

Step 1: Unbox and Inspect

  • Check all parts against the included parts list
  • Inspect for shipping damage (bent rods, cracked parts)
  • Read the quick start guide — seriously, read it

Step 2: Assembly

  1. Assemble the frame per instructions (most printers are 80% pre-assembled)
  2. Check all screws — Tighten everything. Factory assembly is often loose
  3. Install the Z-axis gantry — Make sure it moves smoothly up and down
  4. Attach the extruder and filament holder

Step 3: Level the Bed

This is the most critical step. Follow our detailed leveling guide, but the quick version:

  1. Heat the bed to printing temperature (60°C for PLA)
  2. Disable steppers (Motors Off)
  3. Use the paper test at each corner — you should feel slight drag
  4. Re-check all corners 2-3 times

Step 4: Load Filament and Test

  1. Feed filament into the extruder until it comes out the nozzle
  2. Print the included test file (usually a small cat or calibration cube)
  3. Watch the first layer carefully

For detailed help, see our printer guide and beginner mistakes guide.
