HIPS Filament Guide: The Perfect Dissolvable Support Material

📅 June 1, 2026⏱️ 6 min read✍️ 3D Print Starter Team
🧪

HIPS (High Impact Polystyrene) is a unique filament that serves a specific purpose: it's the best dissolvable support material for ABS prints. While most makers use breakaway supports, HIPS opens up possibilities for complex geometries that would be impossible to clean up manually.

How HIPS Works as Support Material

HIPS dissolves in d-Limonene (citrus solvent), while ABS is unaffected. This means you can print a model in ABS with HIPS supports, then dissolve away the HIPS in a d-Limonene bath — leaving perfectly clean surfaces even in internal cavities and overhangs.

Key Settings

SettingValue
Nozzle Temperature220-250 °C
Bed Temperature90-100 °C
Print Speed40-60 mm/s
Part Cooling0-20%
Interface Layers2-3 (critical for clean separation)

Dissolving HIPS

  1. Fill a glass container with d-Limonene
  2. Submerge the printed part
  3. Wait 12-24 hours (agitation speeds up the process)
  4. Rinse thoroughly with warm soapy water

Safety note: d-Limonene is a skin irritant. Wear gloves and work in a ventilated area.
