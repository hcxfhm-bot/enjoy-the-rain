3D Print Surface Finish Comparison: Matte, Glossy & Textured

📅 June 1, 2026⏱️ 6 min read✍️ 3D Print Starter Team
🎨

The surface finish of your 3D print affects both its appearance and functionality. A glossy finish looks great on display models but shows every imperfection. A matte finish hides flaws but may look dull. And textured finishes can add grip or visual interest.

Finish Types Compared

FinishLookBest ForHow to Achieve
MatteFlat, even, no shineFunctional parts, mechanical componentsMatte filament, sanding to 600 grit, matte clear coat
GlossyShiny, reflectiveDisplay models, decorative piecesGlossy filament, epoxy coating, vapor smoothing
TexturedPatterned surfaceHandles, cosplay props, artistic piecesTextured PEI plate, embossed patterns, sandblast
SatinSemi-gloss, soft sheenJewelry, consumer productsSanding to 1000 grit + satin clear coat

How Your Build Plate Affects Finish

The bottom of your print takes on the texture of your build plate:

  • Smooth PEI → Glossy bottom
  • Textured PEI → Matte/textured bottom (great for visible bottom surfaces)
  • Glass plate → Mirror finish bottom
  • Garolite → Textured, matte finish
