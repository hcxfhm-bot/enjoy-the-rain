Anycubic Kobra 3 Review: Budget Speed Demon

📅 June 1, 2026 ⏱️ 7 min read ✍️ 3D Print Starter Team
🐍

Anycubic has been quietly improving its Kobra lineup, and the Kobra 3 represents a significant step forward. With Klipper firmware, high-speed printing, and an attractive price point, it's aimed squarely at budget-conscious makers who want speed without paying Bambu prices.

Key Specs

SpecKobra 3
Build Volume250 × 250 × 260 mm
Max Speed600 mm/s
Acceleration20,000 mm/s²
ExtruderDirect Drive
Auto Bed LevelingAnycubic LeviQ (inductive)
FirmwareKlipper (AnycubicOS)
Multi-ColorACE Pro compatible (4 colors)
Price~$299

The ACE Pro Multi-Color System

The standout feature is the ACE Pro (Anycubic Color Engine Pro), an optional multi-color unit at ~$200. Unlike Bambu's AMS which uses a spool-switching approach, the ACE Pro can blend up to 4 colors. It's not as refined as the AMS, but at half the price, it's a compelling option for budget multi-color printing.

Print Quality and Speed

At 200-300 mm/s, the Kobra 3 produces clean, consistent prints. The Klipper-based firmware provides input shaping and pressure advance, eliminating the ringing artifacts that older Marlin-based Anycubic printers suffered from.

At 500+ mm/s, quality degrades — more visible on larger models. Stick to 200-300 mm/s for display pieces and reserve higher speeds for functional drafts.

Strengths

  • Excellent value at $299 with Klipper and direct drive
  • ACE Pro multi-color at half the price of Bambu's AMS
  • LeviQ auto leveling is fast and reliable
  • Active Anycubic community and regular firmware updates

Weaknesses

  • ACE Pro has more filament jams than AMS — not as reliable for unattended multi-color
  • No enclosure option — ABS/ASA printing requires DIY solutions
  • Build quality is acceptable but not premium; some units need belt tensioning out of the box
  • Documentation and support lag behind Creality and Bambu

Verdict

The Kobra 3 is the best budget speed printer if you want Klipper out of the box without paying Bambu prices. The ACE Pro adds real multi-color capability at an unmatched price. Just temper your expectations on polish — this is a value play, not a premium experience.
