How to Price Your 3D Prints: Formulas, Tips & Profit Margins

📅 June 1, 2026⏱️ 7 min read✍️ 3D Print Starter Team
💵

Pricing is where most 3D printing businesses fail. Price too high and you lose customers. Price too low and you lose money on every print. Here's a practical framework for setting profitable prices.

The Pricing Formula

Price = (Material Cost + Machine Time + Labor + Overhead) × Markup

Breaking Down Each Component

ComponentHow to CalculateExample
Material CostWeight × cost per kg50g × $22/kg = $1.10
Machine TimePrint hours × hourly rate ($1-3/hr)4 hrs × $2 = $8.00
LaborSetup + removal + post-processing30 min × $15/hr = $7.50
Overhead10-20% of subtotal$16.60 × 15% = $2.49
Markup×2-3 for retail$19.09 × 2.5 = $47.73

Common Pricing Mistakes

  • Forgetting labor — Your time has value. Include setup, monitoring, and finishing
  • Ignoring electricity — A 300W printer running 10 hours = $0.40 (small but adds up)
  • No markup for profit — Cost ≠ price. You need margin for growth
  • Racing to the bottom — Competing on price alone is a losing game
