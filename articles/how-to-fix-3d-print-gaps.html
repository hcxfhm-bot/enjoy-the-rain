How to Fix Gaps in 3D Prints: Under-Extrusion Solutions

📅 June 1, 2026⏱️ 7 min read✍️ 3D Print Starter Team
🕳️

Gaps in your print — whether between perimeters, in the top layers, or inside infill — all trace back to one root cause: not enough filament is being deposited where it should be. Here's how to find and fix the specific cause.

Gap Types and Causes

Gap LocationMost Likely CauseFirst Fix to Try
Between wallsFlow rate too lowIncrease flow to 95-105%
Top surface holesNot enough top layersIncrease top layers to 6+
Infill gapsSpeed too high for extrusion rateReduce infill speed
First layer gapsNozzle too far from bedLower Z-offset by 0.05mm
Random gapsPartial clog or wet filamentCold pull or dry filament

Systematic Fix Approach

  1. Calibrate E-steps — Measure actual vs commanded extrusion. See our E-steps guide
  2. Increase flow rate — Try +5% increments until gaps close
  3. Check filament diameter — Measure with calipers. If 1.70mm instead of 1.75mm, your slicer is under-extruding
  4. Dry your filament — Wet filament bubbles and steams, reducing effective extrusion
  5. Reduce print speed — If the extruder can't keep up, slow down

For more help, see our under-extrusion guide and filament drying guide.
