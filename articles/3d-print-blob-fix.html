How to Fix Blobs and Zits on 3D Prints: Settings Guide

📅 June 1, 2026⏱️ 6 min read✍️ 3D Print Starter Team
🔵

Blobs and zits are small raised imperfections that appear on the surface of your print, typically where the nozzle starts or stops extruding. They're caused by excess filament oozing out at the wrong moment.

Main Causes

  • Insufficient retraction — Filament oozes during travel moves
  • Coasting too aggressive — Slightly reduces extrusion at the end of a line, but too much causes gaps
  • Pressure in the nozzle — Residual pressure pushes filament out after extrusion stops
  • Wipe settings — "Wipe while retracting" can help if configured correctly

Settings to Adjust

SettingChangeEffect
Retraction DistanceIncrease by 0.5mmPulls more filament back, reduces oozing
Retraction Speed20-40 mm/sFaster retraction = less time for ooze
Extra Prime AmountSet to -0.063mm³Compensates for filament lost during retraction
CoastingEnable at 0.2-0.4mm³Uses nozzle pressure to finish the line without extruding
Wipe While RetractingEnableMoves nozzle inward while retracting to clean tip
