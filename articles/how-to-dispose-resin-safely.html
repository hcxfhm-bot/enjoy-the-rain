How to Dispose of 3D Printing Resin Safely: EPA Guidelines

📅 June 1, 2026⏱️ 6 min read✍️ 3D Print Starter Team
♻️

Resin is classified as hazardous waste. Pouring it down the drain is illegal in most jurisdictions and contaminates water systems. Here's how to dispose of it properly.

Disposal by Resin State

Uncured Liquid Resin

  • Never pour down any drain
  • Cure it first: pour into a clear container, leave in direct sunlight for 1-2 hours
  • Once fully cured (solid, no tackiness), it can go in regular trash

Resin-Contaminated IPA

  • Leave in sunlight to cure suspended resin particles
  • Strain out cured solids (dispose in regular trash)
  • Remaining IPA can evaporate in a well-ventilated area
  • Never pour contaminated IPA down the drain

Cured Resin Prints

  • Fully cured prints are non-toxic and can go in regular trash
  • They are NOT recyclable — put in general waste

Failed Prints and Supports

  • Cure in sunlight before disposal
  • Once cured, regular trash

Local Regulations

Some municipalities classify uncured resin as hazardous waste requiring special disposal. Check your local EPA equivalent for specific rules. When in doubt, cure everything before throwing it away.
