Klipper Pressure Advance: Perfect Extrusion Every Time

📅 June 1, 2026⏱️ 7 min read✍️ 3D Print Starter Team
🎯

Pressure Advance (PA) is Klipper's solution to extrusion inconsistency. Without PA, corners get bulges (too much filament) and straight sections after corners get thin (too little). PA compensates for the pressure dynamics in your hotend, producing perfectly consistent extrusion.

How Pressure Advance Works

When the extruder pushes filament, pressure builds up in the nozzle. When the extruder stops or slows down, that pressure continues pushing filament out. PA anticipates this by starting and stopping extrusion slightly before the nozzle reaches its target, so the actual filament output matches the intended path exactly.

Calibration Method

  1. Start with a low value: Set pressure_advance: 0.0 in your config
  2. Print a PA test pattern: A square tower with TUNING_TOWER COMMAND=SET_PRESSURE_ADVANCE PARAMETER=ADVANCE START=0 FACTOR=0.02
  3. Inspect the result: Find the height where corners look cleanest — no bulging and no gaps
  4. Calculate the value: Height × FACTOR = your PA value
  5. Typical values: Direct drive 0.02-0.08, Bowden 0.3-0.8

Signs PA Needs Adjustment

SymptomPA Too LowPA Too High
CornersBulgingUnder-extruded
Start of linesNormalGaps/blob-free
End of linesOozingThin/starved
