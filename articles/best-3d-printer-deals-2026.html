3D printer prices fluctuate throughout the year, with some deals saving you 20-40%. Here's when and where to buy for maximum savings.

Best Times to Buy

PeriodTypical DiscountNotes
Black Friday / Cyber Monday20-40%Best deals of the year
Amazon Prime Day (July)15-30%Good but less selection

Best Retailers by Brand

  • Creality: Official store (AliExpress) + Amazon
  • Bambu Lab: Official store only (rarely discounted)
  • Prusa: Official store (no discounts, ever)
  • Elegoo/Anycubic: AliExpress official + Amazon

Red Flags: Not a Real Deal

  • Inflated "was" price — Check price history on CamelCamelCamel
  • Unknown brands with suspicious specs — Stick to established brands
  • "Limited time" pressure tactics — Deals come around regularly
