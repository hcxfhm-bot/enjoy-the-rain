Tinkercad for 3D Printing: Design Your First Model in 30 Minutes

📅 June 1, 2026⏱️ 6 min read✍️ 3D Print Starter Team
🧱

Tinkercad is a browser-based 3D design tool that requires zero installation and zero experience. If you can drag and drop, you can design printable models. Here's your fast-track tutorial.

Getting Started

  1. Go to tinkercad.com → Create Account (free)
  2. Click "Create New Design"
  3. You'll see a workplane with a grid and a panel of shapes on the right

Basic Operations

  • Add shape: Drag from right panel to workplane
  • Resize: Click and drag white handles
  • Move: Click and drag the shape
  • Raise/lower: Click the black arrow above the shape
  • Hole mode: Select shape → change from "Solid" to "Hole" → Group with another shape to cut it
  • Rotate: Use the rotation arrows

Quick Project: Custom Keychain

  1. Drag a rounded rectangle to the workplane (60mm × 25mm × 3mm)
  2. Drag a cylinder (hole, 5mm diameter) to the top-left corner for the keyring hole
  3. Drag text shape → type your name → make it 2mm tall
  4. Position text on the rectangle
  5. Select all → Group
  6. Export as STL → Print!

Exporting

Click Export → STL → Download. Import into your slicer and print. It's that simple.
