DIY 3D Printer Enclosure: Build One for Under $50

📅 June 1, 2026⏱️ 7 min read✍️ 3D Print Starter Team
🏠

An enclosure is essential for ABS/ASA printing and helpful for noise reduction, draft prevention, and fume containment. Commercial enclosures cost $100-300, but you can build one for under $50.

Method 1: IKEA Lack Table Stack (~$30)

The classic DIY enclosure. Stack 2-3 IKEA Lack tables ($10 each), add acrylic panels to the sides, and you have a perfectly sized enclosure for most Ender 3/A1 class printers.

Method 2: Wire Shelving + Foam Board (~$40)

Use a wire shelving unit as the frame, line with foam board insulation, and add acrylic doors. More space, better insulation, slightly more effort.

Critical Design Elements

  • Ventilation — Add a HEPA/activated carbon filter with a 120mm PC fan
  • Fire safety — Use a thermal fuse or smart plug with auto-off
  • Cable routing — Plan for power and USB cable exits
  • Spool holder — Mount outside the enclosure to avoid heat-softening filament
  • Temperature monitoring — A cheap digital thermometer inside
