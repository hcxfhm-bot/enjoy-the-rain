Spring Mods for 3D Printers: Bed Springs, Extruder Springs & More

📅 June 1, 2026⏱️ 6 min read✍️ 3D Print Starter Team
🔧

Stock springs on budget printers are often too soft, leading to bed leveling drift and extruder slipping. Simple spring upgrades are cheap and effective.

Spring Upgrade Options

SpringProblemUpgradeCost
Bed leveling springsBed drifts out of levelStiffer yellow springs or silicone mounts$5-8
Extruder arm springGear slipping on filamentStiffer spring from upgrade kit$3-5
Blower fan dampersVibration noiseRubber grommets or TPU mounts$2-4

Silicone Bed Mounts vs Yellow Springs

Silicone mounts are the most popular bed spring replacement in 2026. They're heat-resistant, don't compress over time, and maintain level for months. Yellow springs are stiffer than stock but still metal and can drift. Silicone mounts win for long-term stability.
