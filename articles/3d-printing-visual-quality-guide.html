How to Achieve Visual-Perfect 3D Prints: Settings for Display Models

📅 June 1, 2026⏱️ 7 min read✍️ 3D Print Starter Team
✨

Functional prints and display prints have different priorities. When the print needs to look perfect — miniatures, figurines, art pieces — you need different settings than for functional parts.

Visual-Quality Settings

SettingValueWhy
Layer Height0.08-0.12mmFiner layers = less visible lines
Nozzle0.2mmFiner detail, thinner lines
Speed20-30 mm/sSlower = cleaner extrusion
Perimeters3-4Stronger, smoother walls
Infill10-15% GyroidJust enough support for top surfaces
Top Layers6-8Completely flat, smooth top
Outer Wall Speed15-20 mm/sSlowest, cleanest surface
IroningEnabled (top surfaces)Smooths flat top surfaces
Seam PositionAligned or RearHides Z-seam on the back

Post-Processing for Display

  1. Sand with 220 → 400 → 800 → 2000 grit
  2. Fill any gaps with spot putty
  3. Prime with filler primer
  4. Sand primer smooth
  5. Paint with model paints
  6. Apply clear coat (matte or gloss)

For more finishing tips, see our sanding guide and painting guide.
