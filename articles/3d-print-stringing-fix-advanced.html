Advanced Stringing Fix: When Basic Retraction Isn't Enough

📅 June 1, 2026⏱️ 7 min read✍️ 3D Print Starter Team
🕸️

You've adjusted retraction distance and speed, but stringing persists. This means the problem isn't simple retraction — it's one of several advanced causes.

Beyond Basic Retraction

1. Temperature Too High

The #1 overlooked cause. If the nozzle is too hot, filament oozes regardless of retraction. Try reducing by 5°C increments until stringing stops. For PLA: try 195-200°C instead of 210°C.

2. Enable Coasting

Coasting stops extrusion slightly before the end of a line, using residual nozzle pressure to finish. This reduces the pressure that causes ooze during travel. Set coasting volume to 0.2-0.6mm³.

3. Enable Wipe While Retracting

The nozzle moves inward over the already-printed perimeter while retracting, wiping the tip clean. Very effective for PETG stringing.

4. Increase Travel Speed

Faster travel = less time for ooze. Set travel speed to 200-300mm/s. Just make sure your printer can handle it without skipping steps.

5. Firmware: Linear Advance / Pressure Advance

These firmware features manage nozzle pressure dynamically, preventing ooze at a fundamental level. See our Pressure Advance guide.

6. Dry Your Filament

Wet filament produces steam, which increases ooze dramatically. Even if the filament is new, it may have absorbed moisture during shipping. Dry at 50°C for 4-6 hours.
