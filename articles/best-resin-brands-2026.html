Best 3D Printing Resin Brands in 2026: Standard, Flexible & Castable

📅 June 1, 2026⏱️ 7 min read✍️ 3D Print Starter Team
🧪

Choosing the right resin is just as important as choosing the right printer. The wrong resin can cause failed prints, poor detail, or safety hazards. Here are the best resin brands by category in 2026.

Best Standard Resins

  • Anycubic Grey/White — Best value, reliable, low odor ($25/kg)
  • Elegoo Standard Grey — Excellent detail, fast cure ($22/kg)
  • Siraya Tech Simple — Low odor, easy to wash, great for beginners ($30/kg)

Best Engineering Resins

  • Siraya Tech Sculpt/Ultra — High strength, heat resistant
  • 3DM ABS-like — Impact resistant, good for functional parts
  • Liquire Cast — Investment casting, burns out cleanly

Best Flexible Resins

  • Liquire Flexible — 60A shore hardness, good elasticity
  • 3DM Flex — Softer at 40A, great for gaskets
