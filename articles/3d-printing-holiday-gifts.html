3D Printed Holiday Gifts: 20 Ideas for Every Occasion

📅 June 1, 2026⏱️ 7 min read✍️ 3D Print Starter Team
🎁

3D printed gifts show thought and effort that store-bought items can't match. Here are 20 ideas organized by occasion, from quick prints to impressive showpieces.

Quick Prints (Under 2 Hours)

  • Custom keychain with recipient's name
  • Phone stand with personalized message
  • Cookie cutter in a fun shape
  • Desk organizer / pen holder
  • Guitar pick with custom design

Medium Prints (2-6 Hours)

  • Lithophane photo (backlit portrait)
  • Flexi articulated toy (fish, dinosaur)
  • Custom coasters with monogram
  • Puzzle box with secret compartment
  • Magnetic picture frame

Impressive Prints (6+ Hours)

  • Vase mode decorative vase
  • Board game with custom pieces
  • Functional lamp with integrated design
  • Topographic map of a meaningful location
  • Articulated armor or costume piece

Tips for Gift Prints

  • Use premium filament — Matte, silk, or color-shift PLA looks expensive
  • Sand and paint for a finished look
  • Include a card explaining it's 3D printed
  • Package nicely — A plain print in a nice box beats a great print in a ziplock
