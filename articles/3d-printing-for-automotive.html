3D Printing for Automotive: Parts, Repairs & Custom Mods

📅 June 1, 2026⏱️ 7 min read✍️ 3D Print Starter Team
🚗

The automotive industry was an early adopter of 3D printing, and in 2026 the technology is accessible to everyone from professional mechanics to weekend tinkerers. Here's how to use 3D printing for automotive applications.

What You Can (and Can't) 3D Print for Cars

Part TypeFeasible?Best MaterialNotes
Interior trim/covers✅ YesPETG, ABSNot structural, heat moderate
Custom brackets/mounts✅ YesPETG, Nylon, PCVerify load requirements
Cable organizers✅ YesPLA, PETGLow stress, easy prints
Engine components❌ No—Too hot, too much vibration
Brake components❌ No—Safety-critical, never 3D print
Exterior body panels⚠️ LimitedASA, PCUV and heat resistant materials only

Heat Resistance Is Key

Under-hood temperatures can reach 120°C. Interior dash temperatures in direct sun can hit 90°C. PLA (Tg 60°C) will deform in a hot car. Use ABS, ASA, PETG, or PC for anything that experiences heat.

For material guidance, see our PETG vs PLA guide and ASA filament guide.
