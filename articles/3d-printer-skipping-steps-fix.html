3D Printer Skipping Steps: Motor and Belt Troubleshooting

📅 June 1, 2026⏱️ 7 min read✍️ 3D Print Starter Team
⚙️

Skipped steps happen when the stepper motor can't generate enough torque to move the axis, causing it to jump positions. The result is shifted layers, distorted geometry, and ruined prints. Here's how to fix it.

Symptoms of Skipped Steps

  • Print suddenly shifts in one axis direction
  • Layer lines don't align vertically
  • Audible "thunk" or grinding sound during printing
  • The print is skewed (not rectangular when it should be)

Common Causes and Fixes

1. Loose Belts

The most common cause. If belts are slack, the motor turns but the axis doesn't move the full distance.

Fix: Tighten belts until they twang like a guitar string when plucked. Check our belt tension guide.

2. Stepper Driver Current Too Low

If the driver doesn't supply enough current, the motor lacks torque under load.

Fix: Increase VREF by 10-20% (carefully — too much current overheats the motor). Typical values: X/Y 0.8-1.0V, Z 0.6-0.8V, E 0.5-0.7V.

3. Print Speed Too High

At very high speeds, motors can't accelerate fast enough and skip steps.

Fix: Reduce print speed by 25% and see if the problem resolves. Also reduce acceleration values.

4. Mechanical Binding

If an axis is physically hard to move (bent rods, misaligned rails, overtight wheels), the motor has to work harder and may skip.

Fix: Power off the printer and move each axis by hand. They should glide smoothly with consistent resistance. If any axis is stiff, investigate the cause.

For more help, see our layer shift guide and belt tension guide.
