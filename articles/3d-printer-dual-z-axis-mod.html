Dual Z-Axis Mod: Why You Need It and How to Install

📅 June 1, 2026⏱️ 7 min read✍️ 3D Print Starter Team
📏

If your printer has a single Z-axis lead screw, your X-axis gantry can sag on one side, causing uneven first layers and inconsistent print quality across the bed. A dual Z-axis mod adds a second lead screw and motor for perfectly level gantry movement.

Signs You Need Dual Z

  • First layer is thicker on one side of the bed
  • Prints lean slightly to one side on taller models
  • You have to re-level the bed frequently
  • Gantry droops when you push down on the right side

Installation Options

MethodCostDifficultyNotes
Second motor + lead screw kit$15-25ModerateMost common, requires firmware change
Belt-driven second screw$10-15ModerateUses existing motor, no firmware change
Motorized gantry leveling$30-40AdvancedIndependent Z motors with auto-leveling
