3D Printing in Education: How Schools Use Printers in 2026

📅 June 1, 2026⏱️ 7 min read✍️ 3D Print Starter Team
🎓

3D printing has moved from novelty to necessity in education. Schools across the world are integrating printers into curricula from elementary through university level. Here's how they're using them — and what you need to know if you're considering adding a printer to your classroom.

Subject Applications

SubjectHow 3D Printing Is Used
STEMEngineering prototypes, physics models, chemistry molecules
Art & DesignSculpture, jewelry, product design prototyping
HistoryHistorical artifacts, architectural models
MathematicsGeometric shapes, topology models, fractals
VocationalManufacturing skills, CAD/CAM training, entrepreneurship

Best Printers for Schools

  • Enclosed printers preferred — No exposed hot parts for student safety
  • HEPA filtration — Essential for indoor classroom use
  • Easy to use — Teachers shouldn't need to be 3D printing experts

See our school printer guide for specific recommendations.

Safety Considerations

  • Only PLA in classrooms — no ABS/ASA (toxic fumes)
  • Enclosed printers with auto-shutoff
  • Supervision required for students under 12
  • HEPA filter running during all prints
