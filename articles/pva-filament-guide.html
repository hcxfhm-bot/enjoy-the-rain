PVA Filament Guide: Water-Soluble Supports Explained

📅 June 1, 2026⏱️ 7 min read✍️ 3D Print Starter Team
💧

PVA (Polyvinyl Alcohol) filament is a water-soluble support material that dissolves in plain water. It's the easiest dissolvable support to use — no chemicals needed, no special handling, just soak and wait. But it comes with its own quirks that you need to understand.

When to Use PVA

PVA is ideal for prints with complex internal cavities, intricate overhangs, or trapped supports that can't be removed mechanically. Common use cases:

  • Internal channels for fluid flow models
  • Honeycomb and lattice structures
  • Complex geometric art pieces
  • Prototype molds with internal features

Key Settings

SettingValue
Nozzle Temperature185-200 °C
Bed Temperature45-60 °C (PEI or PVA sheet)
Print Speed30-50 mm/s
Part Cooling50-70%
StorageMust be kept dry (absorbs moisture rapidly)

Dual Extrusion Setup

PVA requires a dual-extrusion printer. One nozzle prints your model material (typically PLA), and the other prints PVA supports. Key slicer settings:

  • Set PVA as support interface material only (not all support material) to save cost
  • Support Z-distance: 0.2 mm — creates a gap for easy water penetration
  • Support interface layers: 2-3 for clean removal

Dissolving PVA

  1. Submerge the print in warm water (40-60°C works best)
  2. Wait 2-8 hours depending on support volume
  3. Agitate occasionally to speed up dissolution
  4. Rinse with clean water when done
