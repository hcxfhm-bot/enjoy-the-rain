3D Printing vs Injection Molding: When to Use Each in 2026

📅 June 1, 2026⏱️ 7 min read✍️ 3D Print Starter Team
🏭

3D printing and injection molding aren't competitors — they're complementary. Each excels at different production volumes and requirements. Here's how to choose.

Comparison

Factor3D PrintingInjection Molding
Setup Cost$0$5,000-100,000+
Per-Part Cost (1 unit)$0.50-20N/A (mold cost dominates)
Per-Part Cost (10,000 units)$0.50-20$0.05-2
Crossover Point—Usually 500-2000 units
Lead TimeHours4-12 weeks
Design FreedomUnlimitedLimited by mold constraints
Material Options100+ filament typesVirtually any plastic
Surface FinishLayer lines visibleGlossy, smooth, professional

When to 3D Print

  • Prototyping and design iteration
  • Production under 500 units
  • Custom/personalized items
  • Complex geometries that can't be molded
  • Fast time-to-market requirements

When to Injection Mold

  • Production over 2,000 units
  • Consistent quality across all parts
  • Specific engineering plastics needed
  • Consumer product with surface finish requirements
