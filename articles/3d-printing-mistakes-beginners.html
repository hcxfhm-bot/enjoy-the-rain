Top 15 Mistakes Beginners Make in 3D Printing (And How to Avoid Them)

📅 June 1, 2026⏱️ 9 min read✍️ 3D Print Starter Team
⚠️

Every experienced 3D printer operator made these mistakes when starting out. Learn from our failures so you don't have to repeat them.

The Top 15 Mistakes

  1. Not leveling the bed first — Everything else is pointless if the bed isn't level
  2. Printing too fast — Slow down. 40-50 mm/s is plenty for your first 20 prints
  3. Using 100% infill — 15-20% with Gyroid is nearly as strong for most parts
  4. Ignoring supports — If in doubt, enable them. Wasted filament beats a 6-hour failed print
  5. Not cleaning the build plate — Wash with dish soap and warm water. Fingerprints kill adhesion
  6. Changing multiple settings at once — Change one thing, test, change another. Otherwise you can't diagnose problems
  7. Using the wrong filament settings — PETG needs different temps than PLA. Double-check before printing
  8. Skipping the first layer watch — Watch at least the first layer. 90% of failures start there
  9. Not drying filament — Wet filament causes stringing, popping, and weak prints. Even "new" filament can be wet
  10. Printing tall, thin objects without a brim — They fall over. Always add a brim
  11. Using cheap SD cards — Corrupted G-code = corrupted print. Use a quality Class 10 card
  12. Not calibrating E-steps — If your extruder isn't pushing the right amount of filament, nothing else matters
  13. Designing without tolerances — 3D prints aren't perfectly dimensionally accurate. Add 0.2mm clearance for fits
  14. Leaving the printer unattended for long prints — Check periodically. A failed 12-hour print wastes time and filament
  15. Giving up after a few failures — Every failure teaches something. Keep a log and learn from each one
