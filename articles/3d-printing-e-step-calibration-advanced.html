Advanced Extruder Calibration: Flow Rate, Pressure Advance & More

📅 June 1, 2026⏱️ 7 min read✍️ 3D Print Starter Team
🎯

Basic E-step calibration gets you 90% of the way there. The last 10% — flow rate, pressure advance, and linear advance — separates good prints from perfect ones.

Flow Rate Calibration

Flow rate (also called extrusion multiplier) fine-tunes how much filament is extruded relative to what the slicer commands. Even with perfect E-steps, different filaments flow differently.

  1. Print a single-wall hollow cube (0% infill, 1 perimeter)
  2. Measure the wall thickness with calipers
  3. If wall is 0.45mm but should be 0.40mm: flow = 0.40/0.45 = 0.889 → Set flow to 89%

Pressure Advance (Klipper)

PA compensates for nozzle pressure dynamics. Without it, corners get bulges and straight sections after corners get thin. See our full PA guide.

Linear Advance (Marlin)

Marlin's equivalent of Pressure Advance. Enable with M900 K[value]. Typical values: 0.0-0.1 for direct drive, 0.2-0.6 for Bowden. Test with the Marlin linear advance test pattern.

Putting It All Together

  1. Calibrate E-steps first
  2. Then calibrate flow rate
  3. Then tune pressure/linear advance
  4. Print a calibration cube to verify

For more calibration, see our calibration guide and E-steps guide.
