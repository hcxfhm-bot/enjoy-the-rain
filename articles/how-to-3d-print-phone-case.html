How to 3D Print a Phone Case: Design Tips & Settings

📅 June 1, 2026⏱️ 6 min read✍️ 3D Print Starter Team
📱

A custom phone case is one of the most practical things you can 3D print. It's personal, functional, and makes a great gift. Here's how to create one that fits perfectly and looks professional.

Design Approach

  • Parametric templates — Search Printables/Thingiverse for your exact phone model. Most popular phones have parametric case generators.
  • Design from scratch — Measure your phone (or find dimensions online) and model in Fusion 360 or Tinkercad
  • Case types: Slim (1.2mm walls), Rugged (2.5mm walls + corner bumpers), Wallet (card slots on back)

Key Design Features

  • Camera cutout — Must be precise. Add 0.5mm clearance around the lens.
  • Button covers — Thin flexible tabs over the power/volume buttons
  • Port cutouts — USB-C and speaker openings at the bottom
  • Inner lip — A 0.5mm raised edge inside the front to protect the screen

Print Settings

SettingValue
MaterialTPU (best) or PETG
Layer Height0.16-0.20 mm
Perimeters3
Infill20% Gyroid
OrientationPrint flat (opening facing up)
SupportsNone needed if designed correctly
