Must-Have Resin 3D Printer Accessories in 2026

📅 June 1, 2026⏱️ 6 min read✍️ 3D Print Starter Team
🧰

Resin printing requires more accessories than FDM. Here's what you actually need (and what you can skip).

Essential Accessories

AccessoryCostPriority
Nitrile gloves (box of 100)$10🔴 Mandatory
IPA (99%) or Wash station$15-80🔴 Mandatory
Silicone mat / work surface$10🟡 Highly recommended
UV curing station$30-80🟡 Highly recommended
FEP replacement film$10-15🟡 Keep a spare
Funnel + filter papers$8🟢 Nice to have
Resin fume extractor$30-100🟡 If printing indoors

Nice-to-Have but Not Essential

  • Magnetic build plate — Easier removal but costs $30+
  • Anti-aliasing screen protector — Marginal quality improvement
  • Automated resin dispenser — Convenient but expensive
