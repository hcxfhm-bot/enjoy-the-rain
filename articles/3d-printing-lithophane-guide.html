How to 3D Print Lithophanes: Photos That Glow in Light

📅 June 1, 2026⏱️ 7 min read✍️ 3D Print Starter Team
🕯️

A lithophane is a 3D-printed image that looks like a plain textured slab from the front, but when you hold it up to light, a detailed photo appears. It's one of the most impressive things you can make with a 3D printer, and it's surprisingly easy.

How Lithophanes Work

Light passes through thin areas more easily than thick areas. By varying the thickness of the print based on the brightness of each pixel in your photo, you create a light-and-shadow image that appears when backlit. Dark areas = thick (blocks light), bright areas = thin (passes light).

Creating a Lithophane

  1. Choose a photo — High contrast photos work best. Faces, landscapes, and silhouettes are ideal.
  2. Convert to lithophane — Use an online tool like 3DP Rocks Lithophane or ItsLitho. Upload your photo, adjust settings, download STL.
  3. Recommended settings: Width 100-150mm, max thickness 3-4mm, positive image

Print Settings (Critical)

Layer Height0.08-0.12 mmFiner layers = more detail = better image
