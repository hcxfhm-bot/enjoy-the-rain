Polycarbonate vs ABS: Which Strong Filament Should You Choose?

📅 June 1, 2026⏱️ 6 min read✍️ 3D Print Starter Team
⚖️

When you need strength from your 3D prints, the two top contenders are polycarbonate (PC) and ABS. Both are serious engineering materials, but they serve different purposes. This comparison helps you choose the right one for your project.

Head-to-Head Comparison

PropertyPolycarbonateABS
Tensile Strength55-75 MPa27-40 MPa
Impact ResistanceVery HighHigh
Heat Deflection Temp130-140 °C90-100 °C
Glass Transition147 °C105 °C
Print DifficultyVery HardModerate
Enclosure RequiredYes (60°C+ chamber)Yes (any enclosure)
Nozzle Temp270-310 °C230-260 °C
Cost per kg$40-80$20-35

When to Choose PC

  • Parts exposed to high temperatures (>100°C)
  • Applications requiring maximum impact resistance
  • Transparent or translucent parts needed
  • Automotive and drone components

When to Choose ABS

  • Functional parts at normal temperatures
  • Budget is a concern (half the price of PC)
  • Easier printing experience
  • Vapor smoothing for smooth finishes
