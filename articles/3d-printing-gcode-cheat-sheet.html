Essential G-Code Commands for 3D Printing: Cheat Sheet

📅 June 1, 2026⏱️ 7 min read✍️ 3D Print Starter Team
📋

You don't need to be a G-code programmer to benefit from knowing a few essential commands. These commands let you manually control your printer, diagnose problems, and perform maintenance.

Movement Commands

CommandWhat It Does
G28Home all axes
G28 X YHome X and Y only (keep Z position)
G1 X100 Y100 Z10 F3000Move to X100 Y100 Z10 at 3000mm/min
G1 Z5 F500Move Z to 5mm (slow, for bed leveling)

Extrusion Commands

CommandWhat It Does
G1 E10 F100Extrude 10mm of filament at 100mm/min
G1 E-5 F300Retract 5mm (for nozzle changes)
M92 E424.9Set E-steps to 424.9
M302 P1Allow cold extrusion (for testing only)

Temperature Commands

CommandWhat It Does
M104 S200Set hotend to 200°C
M140 S60Set bed to 60°C
M105Report current temperatures
M303 E0 S200 C8PID auto-tune hotend at 200°C

Utility Commands

CommandWhat It Does
M500Save settings to EEPROM
M503Print current settings
M84Disable all motors
G29Auto bed level (if ABL installed)
