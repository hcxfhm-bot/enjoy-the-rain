Elegoo Neptune 4 Pro Review: Best Value Under $300?

📅 June 1, 2026 ⏱️ 7 min read ✍️ 3D Print Starter Team
🌊

Elegoo has built a reputation for value in the resin printing space, and the Neptune 4 Pro brings that same philosophy to FDM. At under $300, it offers Klipper firmware, high-speed printing, and a feature set that punches well above its price class.

Key Specs

SpecNeptune 4 Pro
Build Volume225 × 225 × 265 mm
Max Speed500 mm/s
Acceleration10,000 mm/s²
ExtruderDirect Drive (dual-gear)
Auto Bed LevelingInductive probe
FirmwareKlipper
LED StripYes (RGB, addressable)
Price~$279

What Stands Out

1. Klipper at $279

Getting Klipper firmware on a sub-$300 printer is still noteworthy in 2026. It means input shaping, pressure advance, and macro support out of the box — features that used to require hours of custom firmware flashing.

2. Direct Drive Dual-Gear Extruder

The dual-gear extruder grips filament firmly, making it reliable for TPU and other flexibles. No Bowden tube hassles. This is a feature that was premium-only just a year ago.

3. RGB LED Strip

A small but appreciated touch — the built-in LED strip illuminates the build area and can be controlled via G-code. Useful for monitoring prints in low-light conditions and for time-lapse videos.

Real-World Performance

At 200-250 mm/s, the Neptune 4 Pro produces excellent results. Surface quality is clean, dimensional accuracy is within 0.2mm, and the auto bed leveling works consistently.

Pushing to 400+ mm/s reveals the printer's budget nature — more vibration, slight ghosting on sharp corners, and occasional under-extrusion on fast infill. The 10,000 mm/s² acceleration is lower than Bambu or Creality's newer machines, which means it takes longer to reach top speed.

Strengths

  • Best value Klipper printer under $300
  • Direct drive handles all filament types
  • Quick assembly (most users report under 30 minutes)
  • Elegoo's customer support is responsive and helpful

Weaknesses

  • No enclosure — limits ABS/ASA capability
  • Lower acceleration than competitors means less practical high-speed advantage
  • PTFE-lined hotend limits max temperature to ~260°C
  • Smaller community than Ender 3 ecosystem means fewer mods and tutorials

Verdict

If $300 is your hard limit and you want Klipper + direct drive, the Neptune 4 Pro is tough to beat. It's not the fastest or most polished, but it's honest value. For a bit more budget, the Kobra 3 with ACE Pro offers multi-color, and the Ender 3 V3 offers a larger community.
