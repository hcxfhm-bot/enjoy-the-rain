Klipper vs Marlin: Which Firmware Is Better in 2026?

📅 June 1, 2026⏱️ 8 min read✍️ 3D Print Starter Team
⚖️

Marlin has been the default 3D printer firmware for a decade. Klipper is the challenger that's taken the community by storm. Which one should you use in 2026? The answer depends on your priorities.

Feature Comparison

FeatureMarlinKlipper
Input ShapingLimited (some boards)✅ Full support with accelerometer
Pressure AdvanceLinear Advance (limited)✅ Superior PA calibration
Max Practical Speed100-150 mm/s300-600 mm/s
Hardware RequiredJust the printerPrinter + Raspberry Pi
Setup DifficultyEasyModerate
Web InterfaceRequires OctoPrintBuilt-in (Mainsail/Fluidd)
ConfigurationCompile & flashEdit text file (no recompile)
Community SizeVery largeLarge & growing fast

When to Choose Marlin

  • You don't want to buy a Raspberry Pi
  • Your printer works fine and you don't need more speed
  • You prefer a simple, no-extra-hardware setup
  • Your printer has a powerful 32-bit board with TMC drivers

When to Choose Klipper

  • You want the fastest possible print speeds
  • You need input shaping for ringing-free prints
  • You want pressure advance for perfect extrusion
  • You like tweaking settings without recompiling firmware
  • You have a Raspberry Pi available
