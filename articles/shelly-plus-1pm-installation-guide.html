Shelly Plus 1PM Installation Guide: Replace Any Light Switch

📅 July 13, 2026 ⏱️ 8 min read ✍️ 3D Print Starter Team
💡

The Shelly Plus 1PM is one of the most versatile smart relays on the market. Unlike bulky smart switches, this tiny device fits behind any standard light switch and adds WiFi control, power monitoring, and automation capabilities without changing your home's appearance.

In this guide, I'll walk you through the complete installation process—from wiring to configuration—so you can smarten up any light circuit in under 30 minutes.

What Is the Shelly Plus 1PM?

The Shelly Plus 1PM is a 1-channel WiFi relay with power metering. It's the upgraded version of the popular Shelly 1PM, featuring:

  • 16A maximum load — suitable for most residential lighting circuits
  • Built-in power meter — track energy consumption in real-time
  • Works with existing switches — toggle lights manually or via app
  • Native Home Assistant integration — no cloud required
  • Overheating protection — auto-shutoff at 70°C
  • Detached mode — use the switch for scenes while controlling lights independently

Unlike smart bulbs, the Shelly Plus 1PM keeps your existing light fixtures and lets you use any switch style—toggle, paddle, or momentary.

What You'll Need

Before starting, gather these tools and materials:

Required Tools

  • Voltage tester (non-contact or multimeter)
  • Phillips and flathead screwdrivers
  • Wire strippers
  • Needle-nose pliers
  • Electrical tape

Materials

  • Shelly Plus 1PM device
  • Wago connectors or wire nuts (2-3 connectors)
  • Optional: 3D printed enclosure (for outdoor or exposed installations)
💡 Tip: If you're installing multiple Shelly devices, check out our smart home device comparison guide to choose the right hardware for each circuit.

Safety First: Turn Off Power

Before touching any wires, shut off the circuit breaker for the light switch you're working on. Don't rely on the wall switch—always cut power at the panel.

  1. Locate your electrical panel
  2. Flip the breaker for the target circuit to OFF
  3. Use a voltage tester to confirm no power at the switch
  4. Place a piece of tape over the breaker labeled "DO NOT TURN ON"

If you're unsure which breaker controls the switch, turn off the main breaker. It's better to be safe than electrocuted.

Understanding Your Wiring

The Shelly Plus 1PM requires a neutral wire to operate. If your switch box doesn't have a neutral bundle (a group of white wires connected together), you may need to run one or use a different device like the Shelly 1L (which works without neutral but has limitations).

Standard Switch Box Wires

  • Line (Hot) — Usually black, brings power from the breaker
  • Load — Usually black or red, goes to the light fixture
  • Neutral — White wires bundled together
  • Ground — Bare copper or green, for safety

Use your voltage tester to identify the line wire (it'll show voltage when the breaker is on, even with the switch off). The load wire only shows voltage when the switch is ON.

Wiring the Shelly Plus 1PM

The Shelly Plus 1PM has 5 terminals:

  • N — Neutral
  • L — Line (hot)
  • O — Output (to light fixture)
  • I — Input (from switch)
  • SW — Switch terminal

Step-by-Step Wiring

  1. Disconnect the existing switch — Unscrew and pull out the switch, noting which wire was on which terminal
  2. Connect the neutral wire — Join a short white pigtail to the neutral bundle using a Wago connector, then connect it to Shelly's N terminal
  3. Connect the line wire — The incoming hot wire goes to Shelly's L terminal
  4. Connect the load wire — The wire going to your light fixture connects to Shelly's O terminal
  5. Connect the switch — Use two short wires (pigtails) to connect the switch to Shelly's I and SW terminals
  6. Ground the switch — Keep existing ground connections intact

Wiring Diagram (Text Version)

LINE (from breaker) ────────► Shelly L terminal
                              │
                              └────► Switch (one terminal)

NEUTRAL bundle ─────────────► Shelly N terminal

LOAD (to light) ────────────► Shelly O terminal

Switch (other terminal) ────► Shelly SW terminal
⚠️ Warning: Never exceed the 16A maximum load. For high-wattage circuits (like heaters or AC units), use a Shelly Pro series with a contactor. The Plus 1PM is designed for lighting and small appliance loads only.

Physical Installation

Once wired, carefully fold the wires and tuck the Shelly Plus 1PM behind the switch:

  1. Organize wires — Fold neatly to avoid pinching when the switch is mounted
  2. Secure the Shelly — Use the included adhesive pad or zip-ties to prevent rattling
  3. Mount the switch — Screw it back into the box, ensuring no wires get crushed
  4. Install the faceplate — Snap or screw the cover back on

The Shelly Plus 1PM is compact (39 × 36 × 17 mm) and fits in most standard switch boxes. If your box is crowded, consider replacing it with a deeper box during installation.

Initial Configuration

With power restored, it's time to configure the device:

Step 1: Connect to Shelly's WiFi

  1. Open your phone's WiFi settings
  2. Find and connect to ShellyPlus1PM-XXXXXX (the default password is on the device)
  3. Open a browser and go to 192.168.33.1

Step 2: Connect to Your Network

In the Shelly's web interface:

  1. Go to Settings → WiFi
  2. Scan for your home network
  3. Enter your WiFi password
  4. Click Connect

The device will reboot and connect to your network. You can now access it via its IP address (check your router's DHCP table) or through the Shelly Cloud app.

Step 3: Configure Settings

In the Shelly web interface or app, adjust these recommended settings:

  • Button type — Set to "Toggle" for standard switches or "Momentary" for push-button switches
  • Power metering — Enable to track energy usage
  • Auto-on — Optional: automatically turn on at sunset
  • Schedule — Set timers for automatic control

Home Assistant Integration

One of the Shelly Plus 1PM's biggest advantages is native Home Assistant support. Here's how to add it:

Method 1: Discovery (Automatic)

  1. Go to Settings → Devices & Services
  2. Click Add Integration
  3. Search for "Shelly"
  4. Home Assistant will discover the device automatically

Method 2: Manual Configuration

If discovery fails, you can configure it manually:

  1. Find the device's IP address in your router
  2. In Home Assistant, add the Shelly integration with the IP
  3. Optionally, disable cloud access in the Shelly settings for local-only operation

Once integrated, you'll have access to:

  • Light control (on/off)
  • Power consumption sensors
  • Energy total (daily/monthly)
  • Temperature sensor (internal)
  • Overheating alerts

For more Home Assistant automation ideas, see our beginner's automation guide.

Using Detached Mode

Detached mode is a powerful feature that separates the physical switch from the relay output:

  • The switch becomes a scene controller — pressing it can trigger any automation
  • The light can be controlled independently via app, voice, or automation
  • Perfect for "double-tap" scenes or controlling multiple devices

To enable detached mode:

  1. Go to Settings → Input
  2. Set "Button Type" to Detached
  3. Configure actions in Home Assistant using the shelly.click event

Troubleshooting Common Issues

Device Won't Power On

  • Check all wire connections with the breaker off
  • Verify neutral wire is properly connected
  • Use a multimeter to confirm power at the L terminal

Light Won't Turn On

  • Check the load wire connection at the O terminal
  • Verify the bulb isn't burnt out
  • Test the relay in the Shelly app (manual control)

WiFi Connection Drops

  • Move the device closer to your router (metal boxes can block signal)
  • Check for interference from other 2.4GHz devices
  • Set a static IP in your router to prevent DHCP conflicts

Power Metering Shows Zero

  • The load may be too low (under 5W) for accurate measurement
  • Verify the O terminal is connected to the load, not line
  • Check if power metering is enabled in settings
