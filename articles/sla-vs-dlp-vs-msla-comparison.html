SLA vs DLP vs MSLA: Which Resin Tech Is Best in 2026?

📅 June 1, 2026⏱️ 7 min read✍️ 3D Print Starter Team
🔬

Resin 3D printing comes in three main flavors: SLA, DLP, and MSLA. They all use UV light to cure liquid resin layer by layer, but the light source and projection method differ significantly. Here's what you need to know.

Technology Comparison

FeatureSLA (Laser)DLP (Projector)MSLA (LCD)
Light SourceUV laserDLP projectorLED array + LCD
Layer CurePoint by pointWhole layer at onceWhole layer at once
Print SpeedSlowFastFast
XY ResolutionVery high (25-50µm)High (35-50µm)Good (35-50µm typical)
Cost$3,000+$1,500+$150+
MaintenanceLaser alignmentProjector bulbLCD screen (wear item)

Verdict

For 99% of home users and small businesses, MSLA is the right choice. It's affordable, fast, and produces excellent quality. The LCD screen is a wear item (replaces every 1-3 years, $30-80), but at $150-500 for the printer, the economics work overwhelmingly in MSLA's favor. SLA is for industrial dental/jewelry. DLP is a dying category being replaced by MSLA.
