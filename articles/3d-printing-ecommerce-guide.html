Selling 3D Prints Online: E-Commerce Platforms Compared

📅 June 1, 2026⏱️ 7 min read✍️ 3D Print Starter Team
🛒

Choosing the right e-commerce platform can make or break your 3D printing business. Each platform has different fees, audiences, and features. Here's how they compare.

Platform Comparison

PlatformMonthly CostTransaction FeesBest For
Etsy$0.20/listing6.5% + payment processingStarting out, hobbyists
Shopify$29-2990% (just payment processing)Growing businesses, brand building
WooCommerceFree (hosting costs apply)0% + payment processingTechnical users, maximum control
Big CartelFree-$9.990% + payment processingSmall shops, artists
GumroadFree10%Digital models, simple products

Recommendation by Stage

  • Just starting: Etsy — zero upfront cost, built-in audience
  • Growing ($1K+/month): Shopify — better branding, lower fees at scale
  • Technical + want control: WooCommerce — no platform lock-in
  • Digital models only: Gumroad or Cults3D
