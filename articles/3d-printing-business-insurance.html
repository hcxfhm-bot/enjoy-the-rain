3D Printing Business Insurance: What You Need to Know

📅 June 1, 2026⏱️ 6 min read✍️ 3D Print Starter Team
🛡️

Insurance isn't exciting, but it's essential. If a printed part fails and causes injury, or a fire starts from your printer, the financial consequences can be devastating. Here's what coverage you need.

Essential Coverage Types

CoverageWhat It CoversCost/YearDo You Need It?
General LiabilityThird-party injury/property damage$300-800Yes — non-negotiable
Product LiabilityInjury from your printed products$400-1,200Yes — if selling physical prints
PropertyFire, theft, equipment damage$200-600Yes — if you have $2K+ in equipment
Professional LiabilityDesign/advice errors$300-800Maybe — if you consult or design for clients

Key Risk Areas for 3D Printing

  • Fire — Printers run unattended for hours. Thermal runaway is a real risk.
  • Product failure — A printed bracket that breaks under load can cause injury
  • IP infringement — Printing copyrighted designs can lead to lawsuits
  • Toxic fumes — ABS/ASA printing without proper ventilation

Fire Prevention Checklist

  • Install smoke detectors in your printing area
  • Use thermal runaway protection (enabled in firmware)
  • Never print on flammable surfaces
  • Keep a fire extinguisher within reach
  • Use a fused power strip with surge protection
