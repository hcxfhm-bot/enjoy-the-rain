#1: ADXL345 Accelerometer

At $5, this is the single best upgrade for Klipper. It enables automatic input shaping calibration, which eliminates ringing. Without it, you're guessing at shaper values. With it, you get precision-tuned resonance compensation.

#2: All-Metal Hotend

Klipper's speed potential is limited by PTFE-lined hotends that max out at 240°C. An all-metal hotend (Dragon, Mosquito, or Rapido) unlocks 300°C printing for materials like nylon, polycarbonate, and carbon fiber blends.
