How to Fix Z-Banding and Z-Artifacts on 3D Prints

📅 June 1, 2026⏱️ 7 min read✍️ 3D Print Starter Team
📊

Z-banding — those frustrating horizontal lines that appear at regular intervals on your prints — is one of the most common quality issues in FDM printing. The good news: every cause has a clear fix.

Types of Z-Artifacts

TypeAppearancePattern
Z-BandingRegular horizontal linesConsistent spacing matching Z lead screw pitch
Ripple/RingingGhost shadows after featuresFollows sharp corners and details
WobbleShifting/leaning layersGradual or sudden offset

Common Causes and Fixes

1. Loose Z-Axis Rods or Couplers

The Z-axis lead screw and smooth rod must be firmly secured. If either has play, the nozzle moves slightly off-position each layer, creating bands.

Fix: Tighten all set screws on the Z coupler. Ensure the lead screw is straight (roll it on a flat surface to check).

2. Bent Lead Screw

A bent Z lead screw creates a wobble that transfers to the entire X-axis gantry, causing consistent bands at the pitch of the screw (usually 8mm intervals).

Fix: Replace the lead screw or add a flexible coupler that allows the screw to move without transferring wobble to the gantry.

3. Temperature Fluctuations

If the hotend temperature varies by more than ±3°C, extrusion consistency changes, creating subtle bands.

Fix: Enable PID auto-tuning for the hotend: M303 E0 S200 C8 then save with M500.

4. Inconsistent Extrusion

Partially clogged nozzle, worn PTFE tube, or inconsistent filament diameter can all cause periodic banding.

Fix: Clean or replace the nozzle, check the PTFE tube for deformation, and measure filament diameter at several points.
